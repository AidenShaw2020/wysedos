# NTFS-3G UEFI driver

The installer carries the unmodified x64 read/write driver from release 1.9
of Pete Batard's NTFS-3G UEFI port. NTFS-3G is copyright its upstream authors.
The corresponding source archive is included alongside the installer in the
wyseDOS release. It includes the upstream copyright and licence notices and
the build workflows. Individual source files declare their applicable terms,
including GPL-2.0-or-later and GPL-3.0-or-later.

- Release and upstream builds at [NTFS-3G UEFI 1.9](https://github.com/pbatard/ntfs-3g/releases/tag/1.9)
- Source revision [a2226ecd8344a427a7ad79fbb878f3244c7af393](https://github.com/pbatard/ntfs-3g/tree/a2226ecd8344a427a7ad79fbb878f3244c7af393)
- Upstream build instructions in the [source README](https://github.com/pbatard/ntfs-3g/blob/a2226ecd8344a427a7ad79fbb878f3244c7af393/README.md)
