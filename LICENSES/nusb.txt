Licensing boundary
------------------
The checked !README.TXT and _NUSB.INF files identify the package and its
authors but do not provide an explicit blanket redistribution licence for
all included binaries. No such grant, open-source status, public-domain
status or corresponding source code is asserted here. Availability from a
download mirror is provenance, not proof of redistribution permission.
This notice records the limitation; it does not grant additional rights.
Use of Windows components remains subject to their applicable terms.

The installer extracts the pinned originals without patching their files,
keeps the upstream readmes/INF and copies them to C:\DRIVERS\NUSBFE and
C:\DRIVERS\NUSBSE. SHA256.TXT identifies every extracted payload file.
The package is optional for guest Windows; wyseDOS itself does not depend
on installing it. Never use the FE package on SE or the SE package on FE.

The complete packages replace system components, not just USBSTOR. English
files may change parts of a localized installation. FE was tested on this
Wyse; SE remains supplied for a new SE installation, not FE-qualified.
